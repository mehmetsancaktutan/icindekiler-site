Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Yuppa has no user account. Your profile, history and pinned items remain on your device. A product photo is processed only when you request an appraisal.

1. Who operates Yuppa

Yuppa is developed by Mehmet Sancaktutan. Privacy questions may be sent to desteksancaktutan@gmail.com.

2. Data kept on your device

Your optional nickname, allergens, dietary preferences, skin preferences, scan history, pinned items, interface language and notification choices are stored locally. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to iOS backup behaviour.

3. Product appraisal data

When you request an appraisal, the selected product photo, the app language and product details derived from it are sent to Yuppa’s Cloudflare-hosted service. The service forwards the image and relevant product text to Anthropic to identify and assess the product. The service may use web search to corroborate publicly available product information.

4. Service logs, rate limits and caching

Cloudflare may process network information such as an IP address to deliver and protect the service. Yuppa uses coarse network buckets for abuse prevention. Image bytes are not retained by Yuppa after processing; a short-lived cryptographic image fingerprint may be used to avoid duplicate requests. Product appraisal results may be cached without an account identifier to improve speed and control cost.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device, and entitlement status may be managed through RevenueCat. Yuppa does not receive your card details. Apple, Google and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription identifiers under their own privacy policies.

6. What we do not do

Yuppa does not run ads, sell personal data, create an advertising profile, request contacts or precise location, or require an account.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data can be removed from within the app or by deleting the app. Server-side operational records and anonymous product-result caches expire according to security and service-retention rules. Contact us if you need help with a privacy request.

8. Children

Yuppa is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly create accounts or collect account profiles from children.

9. International processing

Cloudflare, Anthropic, Apple and RevenueCat may process data in countries other than yours, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The current revision date appears at the top of this page.
