What the app does

  • Take photos of food and cosmetic products
  • Analyzes the ingredient list and converts it into a clear score
  • Explains additives, sugar, salt, fat, and nutritional values simply
  • Warns you about unsuitable products based on your saved allergen and diet preferences
  • Keeps your scanned products in history, you can add to favorites

Data approach

  • No account. Use without registering or logging in.
  • Your preferences, history, and favorites are stored on your device.
  • Photos and product info are sent to the analysis service for product analysis.
  • No ads, no user tracking.
